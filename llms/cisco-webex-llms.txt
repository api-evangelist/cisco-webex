# Cisco Webex

> Cisco Webex is a collaboration platform for video conferencing, team messaging, calling, devices, and administration. The Webex developer platform exposes REST APIs (base https://webexapis.com/v1), first-party SDKs, embeddable widgets, and official hosted MCP servers. Authentication is OAuth 2.0 (access tokens, personal access tokens, Service Apps, and Bots). This file was generated by API Evangelist from the catalog on 2026-06-20; the provider does not publish a /llms.txt.

## APIs
- [Webex Meetings API](https://developer.webex.com/docs/meetings): Schedule and manage meetings, attendees, and preferences.
- [Webex Messaging API](https://developer.webex.com/docs/messaging): Send/receive messages, manage spaces, files, and adaptive cards.
- [Webex People API](https://developer.webex.com/docs/api/v1/people): User profiles and account administration.
- [Webex Teams API](https://developer.webex.com/docs/api/v1/teams): Teams and team membership.
- [Webex Rooms API](https://developer.webex.com/docs/api/v1/rooms): Spaces (rooms) for collaboration.
- [Webex Memberships API](https://developer.webex.com/docs/api/v1/memberships): Room membership management.
- [Webex Team Memberships API](https://developer.webex.com/docs/api/v1/team-memberships): Team membership management.
- [Webex Webhooks API](https://developer.webex.com/docs/webhooks): Real-time event callbacks (HMAC-signed).
- [Webex Devices API](https://developer.webex.com/docs/api/v1/devices): Device and room-system management.
- [Webex Workspaces API](https://developer.webex.com/docs/api/v1/workspaces): Physical workspaces with devices.
- [Webex Events API](https://developer.webex.com/docs/api/v1/events): Organization activity log.
- [Webex Recordings API](https://developer.webex.com/docs/api/v1/recordings): Meeting recordings and metadata.
- [Webex Converged Recordings API](https://developer.webex.com/docs/api/v1/converged-recordings): Unified Meetings + Calling recordings.
- [Webex Call Controls API](https://developer.webex.com/docs/api/v1/call-controls): Third-party call control for Webex Calling.
- [Webex Attachment Actions API](https://developer.webex.com/docs/api/v1/attachment-actions): Adaptive-card interaction results.
- [Webex Organizations API](https://developer.webex.com/docs/api/v1/organizations): Organization details.
- [Webex Licenses API](https://developer.webex.com/docs/api/v1/licenses): License inventory and assignment.
- [Webex Roles API](https://developer.webex.com/docs/api/v1/roles): Administrative roles.
- [Webex Admin Audit Events API](https://developer.webex.com/docs/api/v1/admin-audit-events): Control Hub audit trail.
